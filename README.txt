Integrated Materials

Extract the entire ZIP, then open index.html in a browser. Keep the audio folder beside index.html.

The eight collapsible unit menus appear first. Reference materials appear in the final collapsible section. Writing guides remain in Reference and include their task numbers. The speaking guide is also linked from Unit 1. Each reading contains Part 1: Reading and Part 2: Vocabulary, with flashcards, a word list, and matching practice. Reading Explorer vocabulary practice appears before Reference. Reading activities are titled Text 1, Text 2, and so on.

The home page uses three-column activity cards in tinted, collapsible unit panels. Fonts and controls follow ENGL010; colors remain distinct.

All 42 original audio files are included. 39 have exact packet-label matches. Three appear as additional recordings; L2.8 and L3.95 are identical audio under different supplied names.

Listening scores follow the supplied packet keys. L7.95 question 7 has no supplied key and is excluded from the score. The keys have not been independently verified against the recordings.

Existing reading answer and flag storage keys are retained. Previous vocabulary progress is carried into each combined reading activity when available in this browser. Local-file storage support depends on the browser.

Source: Integrated Materials V8 by Dan Oxley.
